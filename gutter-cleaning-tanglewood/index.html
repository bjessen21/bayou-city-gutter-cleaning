Tanglewood is a residential enclave near the Galleria, close to Post Oak Boulevard, San Felipe and Woodway Drive. It is known for large lots and a heavily wooded canopy of tall pines and oaks. Many homes are mid-century ranches that have been expanded or replaced with much larger houses, and the roofs and gutters tend to be big and complicated.

Tall pines drop needles year-round, with a big fall peak, plus cones and pollen in spring. Needles are the hardest thing for many gutters to shed, and on larger roofs they gather in valleys and in the low corners of long runs.

When to clean gutters in Tanglewood, Houston, TX

With so many pines overhead, Tanglewood gutters catch debris almost year-round. A May cleaning before hurricane season and a December cleaning after fall needle and leaf drop cover most homes, and many properties here benefit from a third visit in September or October.
