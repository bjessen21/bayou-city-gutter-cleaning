The short answer

Most Houston homes should have gutters cleaned twice a year: once in May and once in December. Heavily treed lots often add a third visit in September or October. Homes with little overhead cover can sometimes stretch to once a year, timed for early May.

Why May and December

  • May. Live oaks have finished shedding, oak and pine pollen has dropped, and you have a cleaning done before June 1, the start of hurricane season. Houston averages nearly 50 inches of rain a year, and the wettest month is typically June.
  • December. Sweetgum, pecan, pine and most deciduous leaves are down. Cleaning then keeps the clutter out of winter rain and puts you in good shape for spring.
  • September or October (optional). If you have a lot of pine, pecan or sweetgum overhead, a fall cleanup keeps gutters flowing through the late-season storms.

Other reasons to clean sooner

  • After a named storm or strong wind. Branches and wet leaf mass are the usual clogs.
  • Water spilling over the edge. If it overflows, the gutter or downspout is blocked now.
  • Plants growing in the gutter. That is a sign debris has been sitting through multiple seasons.
  • Before selling or listing. See gutter cleaning for home sellers.

Mosquitoes and standing water

Gutters that hold water are a common source of backyard mosquitoes. Harris County Public Health’s mosquito guidance tells residents to get rid of standing water around the home, and mosquitoes can develop from egg to adult in about a week or so in warm weather. A gutter that does not drain after rain is worth fixing. See gutter repair.

Common questions

Frequently asked questions

How often should I clean my gutters in Houston?

Twice a year for most homes, in May and December. Heavily treed lots may need a third cleaning in the fall.

Is it better to clean gutters before or after hurricane season?

Before. Clean them in May so they are clear when the first tropical rain arrives, and check again after any significant storm.

Do I need gutter cleaning if I have gutter guards?

Usually yes, about once a year. Guards reduce debris but do not eliminate pollen, needles, moss and roof grit.
