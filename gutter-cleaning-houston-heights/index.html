The Houston Heights was founded in 1891 as one of Texas’ first planned communities, incorporated as its own city in 1896 and annexed by Houston in 1918. It now has three City of Houston historic districts (Heights East, West and South) with design guidelines for exterior changes.

The housing is a patchwork. You will find Victorian cottages and Craftsman bungalows from the early 1900s, renovated and expanded homes, and tall new townhomes built on narrow lots. Pecans, live oaks, sweetgums and crape myrtles line the streets and hang over roofs, so gutters on older homes take leaves, catkins and seed pods while newer townhomes have tight access and tall runs.

How we work in The Heights, Houston, TX

Old gutters, gently

Bayou City Gutter Cleaning cleans homes that already have gutter guards. Guards cut down on debris but they do not end it, and in Houston, with oak pollen, pine needles and heavy summer downpours, most guards need attention at least once a year.

Older Heights homes can have original or early replacement gutters, sometimes on wood fascia that has softened in Houston humidity. We check the fascia before we lean on it and flag rot rather than pull a gutter loose.

When to clean gutters in The Heights, Houston, TX

The Heights has some of Houston’s biggest street trees. Live oaks shed old leaves and long catkins from late winter through spring. Pecans and sweetgums drop leaves, husks and gumballs in the fall. Most Heights homes do well with a May cleaning, ahead of hurricane season, and a December cleaning after the fall drop.
