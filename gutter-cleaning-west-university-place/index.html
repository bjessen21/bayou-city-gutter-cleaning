What gutters deal with in West University Place, Houston, TX

West University Place, usually just called “West U,” incorporated in the 1920s and has stayed a small, tightly built residential city near Rice University and Rice Village. Streets like Buffalo Speedway, Bellaire Boulevard, Rice Boulevard and Kirby Drive border it. Many original bungalows and cottages have been replaced by big two-story houses, but mature live oaks and pecans still shade a lot of roofs.

That produces two kinds of gutter work. Older homes have older gutters and often drainage that was never designed for today’s bigger, hard-surfaced lots. Newer homes have long, tall runs with several roof levels. Flat lots and heavy rain make good downspout discharge especially important here.

How we work in West University Place, Houston, TX

Downspouts and drainage first

West U lots are flat and densely built. We flush every downspout and check extensions and grade so water leaves the house instead of settling next to it.

Big oaks, spring debris

Live oaks shed old leaves and catkins in late winter and spring. Timing a cleaning in May catches the drop before hurricane season.

Tall new builds

Two-story and three-level homes get stabilized ladders or poles and a flow check at the bottom of each downspout.

When to clean gutters in West University Place, Houston, TX

West U’s big oaks make spring the main event: old leaves and long catkin strands fall from about late February through April. Pecans and other trees add a fall drop. Most homes here do well with May and December visits.

Common questions

West University Place gutter cleaning questions

Do you serve the City of West University Place?

Yes. West U is part of our core Inner Loop area.

Why is downspout drainage such an issue in West U?

The lots are flat and heavily built. If a downspout ends near the slab, water has nowhere to go but back toward the foundation. Extensions and clear lines matter.

When is the best time to clean gutters under live oaks?

May, after the spring shed. A second cleaning in December handles the fall drop.
