Who this is for

HOAs and townhome communities

Rows of attached townhomes share gutter runs and downspouts. One clogged run can wet the neighbors.

Landlords and property managers

Tenants rarely clean gutters. A scheduled visit costs less than a water-intrusion call.

Small commercial buildings

Retail strips, offices and small multifamily buildings with accessible eaves.

How recurring service works

  1. Send the list

    Addresses, building type and height, and any access notes. Photos or a site walk help on larger properties.

  2. Get a per-building price

    You receive a clear price for each building, so you can budget by property or by year.

  3. Set a schedule

    Most Houston properties do well with two visits per year: May before hurricane season and December after leaf drop. Heavily treed sites can add a third.

  4. Work and report

    We clean, flush and note damage, then give you a simple list of any repairs to approve.

What property managers usually ask about

  • Consistent timing. The same window each year keeps the wet-season risk low and the budget predictable.
  • Repair flags, not surprise bills. We report problems and you approve the work.
  • Access. Gate codes, lockboxes and tenant notice can all be arranged through the property manager.
  • Consistency across sites. The same method on every building makes it easier to compare condition over time.

Frequently asked questions

How often should an HOA schedule gutter cleaning in Houston?

Twice a year works for most Houston communities: May, before hurricane season, and December, after fall leaf and needle drop. Treed communities may need a third visit.

Do you clean multiple buildings in one visit?

Yes. We group buildings by location to keep visits efficient.

Can you send a report after each visit?

Yes. We can note the buildings cleaned and any repairs we recommend.
