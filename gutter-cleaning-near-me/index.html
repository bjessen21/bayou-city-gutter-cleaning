What a Houston gutter cleaning looks like

How to pick a local gutter cleaner

  • Look for real Houston knowledge. A good crew can tell you what your street drops and when: live oak shed in the spring, pine needles and pecan husks in the fall, magnolia leaves in early summer.
  • Ask how they handle your roof. Tile, slate and standing-seam copper need a different method than asphalt shingles.
  • Ask if downspouts are included. On Houston clay, water that cannot leave the gutter ends up at your slab.
  • Get the price before the work starts. A per-foot price or a written total for the whole house is fair. A vague “starting at” number is not.
