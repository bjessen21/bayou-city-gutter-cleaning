What gutters deal with in Katy, TX

Katy has grown fast along the Katy Freeway (I-10) and the Grand Parkway, with neighborhoods like Cinco Ranch, Seven Meadows and Grand Lakes filling with homes built from the 1990s to today. The land is flat coastal prairie, the trees are young to mid-age, and most homes are two-story houses in HOA communities.

Many of these homes have builder-grade five-inch gutters and small downspouts. In a Houston-area downpour, especially on large or steep roofs, five-inch gutters can overflow even when they are perfectly clean. Katy’s flat lots also mean water has little grade to help it leave. Cleaning helps, but Katy is also a place where an undersized system is a common issue.

How we work in Katy, TX

Clean and flush, then check capacity

We clear the gutters and downspouts, then watch how they handle water. If they overflow when clean, we tell you the system is undersized.

Two-story homes, done safely

Stabilized ladders or poles, and a full flush to confirm the downspouts drain.

Discharge and grade

When to clean gutters in Katy, TX

Younger trees mean lighter debris than older neighborhoods, but pollen, seed pods and shingle granules build up. Most Katy homes do well with a May cleaning before hurricane season, plus December if you have mature oaks or pecans nearby. HOAs often set a schedule for whole streets.

Katy gutter cleaning questions

Why do my Katy gutters overflow even after they are cleaned?

Many builder-installed gutters are five inches wide with small downspouts. That is undersized for some roofs in Houston-area downpours.

Do you clean gutters for Katy HOAs?

Yes. We can price by home or building and set a recurring schedule.

How often should Katy gutters be cleaned?

Most homes do well with a May cleaning and a December cleaning if trees overhang the roof.
