What causes tiger stripes and dark streaks

“Tiger stripes” are the vertical black streaks that run down the front face of white, tan and brown gutters. They are usually a mix of dirt and oxidized runoff from the roof, plus mildew and algae that thrive in Houston’s humidity (average relative humidity is near 75 percent). Once a film forms, it holds more dirt.

Chalky oxidation is a different problem. The finish on older aluminum gutters breaks down under Gulf Coast sun and leaves a dull, powdery surface that rubs off on your hand.

How we clean the outside of gutters

  1. Rinse and loosen

    We rinse the face and downspouts to remove loose dirt and pollen.

  2. Apply a gutter-safe cleaner

    A cleaner formulated for painted aluminum lifts the film without stripping paint.

  3. Soft brush

    Stubborn areas get worked by hand with a soft brush, not a wire brush.

  4. Rinse thoroughly

    Everything is rinsed so runoff does not stain siding or plants.

  5. Check the finish

    If the paint is failing or the metal is deeply oxidized, we tell you washing will not fix it and what will.

Good to know

  • Not every streak comes off. Some stains are etched into the finish.
  • High-pressure washing can dent or strip. Thin aluminum dents and paint can lift, so gentle methods are the safe choice.
  • Do it with a cleaning. Washing the outside while the gutters are being cleaned saves a trip and gives the whole gutter a fresh look.
  • Copper is different. Copper gutters develop a natural patina. Ask before cleaning them. Some owners want to keep it.
Common questions

Frequently asked questions

What are the black streaks on my gutters?

Usually a combination of dirt, roof runoff residue and mildew or algae. In Houston’s humid climate they are very common on light-colored gutters.

Will washing damage my gutters?

Not with the right cleaner and a soft brush. High-pressure washing is riskier and can dent thin metal or lift paint.

Is gutter brightening the same as gutter cleaning?

No. Gutter cleaning clears debris from inside the gutter and downspouts. Brightening cleans the outside surface. Many homeowners do both at once.
