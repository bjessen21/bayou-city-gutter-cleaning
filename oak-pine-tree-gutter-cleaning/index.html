    Special cases

    Homes under pines (The Woodlands, Memorial, Tanglewood)

    Pine needles are thin and long. They pack into valleys, elbows and the hole where the gutter feeds the downspout. Extra attention goes to the outlets and to the roof valleys that feed the gutters.

    Homes under live oaks (Heights, West U, Bellaire, River Oaks)

    Live oaks shed while they put on new leaves, so the mess arrives in spring instead of fall. The catkins are stringy and stick to wet gutters, and pollen film can slow drainage even when it looks like a small amount.

    Roofs with lots of debris

    A roof under heavy tree cover also holds debris that washes into the gutter in the next rain. Blowing off the roof edge at the same visit reduces the second clog. See storm and hurricane season gutter cleanup.

    How often should I clean gutters under live oaks in Houston?

    Twice a year is usually enough: May, after the spring shed, and December. Homes with very dense canopy may add a fall visit.
