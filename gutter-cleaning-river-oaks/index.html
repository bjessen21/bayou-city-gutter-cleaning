What gutters deal with in River Oaks, Houston, TX

River Oaks was laid out in the 1920s by developers Michael Hogg and Hugh Potter on about 1,100 acres, with Hare & Hare as landscape architects. The plan gave the neighborhood its winding, tree-lined streets and esplanades, and it required house designs to be approved by an architect panel. A century later, the houses are big, the lots are landscaped, and the canopy is mature.

That combination shapes the gutter work. Many homes have multiple wings, dormers, valleys and roof levels that funnel water into a few lower gutters. Live oaks, pines and magnolias overhang the roofs, and boxwoods, hedges and planted beds run tight to the foundation, so debris and water both have a lot to land on.

How we work in River Oaks, Houston, TX

Slate, tile and copper are not walked on

Many River Oaks roofs are slate, clay tile or standing-seam metal. These crack or scar under a boot, so we work from ladders, poles and vacuum tools instead of walking them.

Copper and built-in gutters

Copper and lead-coated copper gutters get gentle hand cleaning, not wire brushes or harsh chemicals. Concealed and box gutters are cleaned and flushed and any leaks are noted.

Landscaping is protected

Debris is bagged and removed. We keep ladders off plantings where we can and leave beds and drives as we found them.

When to clean gutters in River Oaks, Houston, TX

River Oaks yards are heavily treed, so gutters see live oak leaves and catkins in spring, pine needles and pollen, and magnolia leaves through early summer. Big roofs collect that debris and wash it into a few low points. Most homes here do well with the two-visit rhythm of May, before hurricane season, and December, after fall leaf drop. Large lots with heavy overhead cover often add a fall visit.

Common questions

River Oaks gutter cleaning questions

Do you work on slate or tile roofs?

We clean gutters on slate and tile homes from ladders and poles and do not walk the roof. If a roof is too delicate or steep to reach safely, we tell you the method before we book.

Do you clean copper gutters?

Yes, by hand and with gentle methods. Copper develops a natural patina and some owners want to keep it, so we ask before doing anything beyond cleaning.

Do I need approval from the River Oaks association to have gutters cleaned?

Routine cleaning does not change your exterior. If you are replacing gutters, changing materials or adding guards, deed restrictions and design review may apply, so check with your association first.
