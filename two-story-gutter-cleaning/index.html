Why tall homes need a different method

Houston builders have been going up for years. Older bungalows next door to new three-story townhomes is normal in Inner Loop neighborhoods, and the new builds often sit on lots so narrow that a full-size extension ladder cannot lean at a safe angle. Two-story homes in Memorial and Tanglewood have the opposite problem: long roof lines and high eaves over landscaped beds you do not want trampled.

Gutter work is one of the more common ways homeowners get hurt on ladders. Getting the method right for the house is the first job, not the cleaning itself.

How we clean higher gutters

Bayou City Gutter Cleaning cleans homes that already have gutter guards. Guards cut down on debris but they do not end it, and in Houston, with oak pollen, pine needles and heavy summer downpours, most guards need attention at least once a year.

Tall-home details that matter in Houston

Fascia condition. Wood fascia in Houston’s humidity can be soft under a gutter. Leaning a ladder on rotten fascia is how gutters get pulled off. We check first.
  • Downspouts that run three stories. A tall downspout builds real water speed. If the bottom section or extension is blocked, it backs up fast. We flush from the top and confirm flow at the bottom.
  • Important. Clean gutters help. They do not repair a foundation. If you see stair-step cracks in brick, sticking doors, sloping floors or gaps around window trim, have a structural engineer inspect the house. We clean and flush gutters and report drainage problems we see, but we do not diagnose foundation damage.Multiple roof levels. Water from an upper roof often drains into a lower gutter. If the lower gutter is clogged, the upper roof effectively dumps onto a blocked system.
  • Valleys and dormers. These concentrate flow. A clog near a valley overflows first in a hard rain, even when the rest of the gutter is fine. Overflow at one spot. A clogged corner or valley sends a heavy stream over the gutter edge in the same place every storm.

  • Price for two- and three-story gutter cleaning

    Two-story homes usually run about $1.05 to $1.90 per linear foot and three-story homes about $1.30 to $2.30, with total jobs often in the $190 to $460 range. Steep pitches commonly add about 15 percent. See the full breakdown in our Houston gutter cleaning cost guide.

    Is it safe to clean two-story gutters myself?

    It can be done, but falls from ladders cause serious injuries every year. Uneven Houston soil after rain, wet fascia and gutters full of wet debris all make it riskier than it looks. Many homeowners hire this out once the house is above one story.

    • Can you clean gutters on a three-story townhome with no side yard?

    Often yes, using long-reach poles or vacuum tools from the ground or a small balcony. Send photos or the address and we will tell you what is possible.
