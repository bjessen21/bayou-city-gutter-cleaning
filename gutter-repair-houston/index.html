The most common gutter problems in Houston

Sagging sections. Weight from packed debris and standing water pulls hangers loose. Gutters need to fall toward the downspout, about a quarter inch for every ten feet. Sagging reverses that.
  • Leaking seams and end caps. Older sectional gutters (joined every ten feet) leak at the joints as sealant dries out in the Texas heat.
  • Bayou City Gutter Cleaning cleans homes that already have gutter guards. Guards cut down on debris but they do not end it, and in Houston, with oak pollen, pine needles and heavy summer downpours, most guards need attention at least once a year.

    When repair is not the answer

    If gutters are cracked in many places, badly corroded or undersized for your roof, repairs only delay the replacement. Builder-grade five-inch gutters on large or steep roofs are a common example: in Houston’s downpours they can overflow no matter how clean they are. In that case, a larger gutter or larger downspouts is the real fix, and we say so.

    Typical Houston gutter repair costs about $240 to $480. Fascia and soffit repairs are a separate cost and depend on how much wood is rotted. See more in the cost guide.

    In a Houston historic district? Changing gutter profile or material on a street-facing side of a home in a designated historic district can involve review by the city’s Historic Preservation office. Repairs in kind are one thing, replacement is another. Check first.

    Why does my gutter hold water after it rains?

    Usually the pitch is wrong or the gutter has sagged. Standing water adds weight and breeds mosquitoes, so it is worth fixing.

    Can you repair a leaking gutter seam?

    S

    Often yes. Clean, dry metal and fresh sealant fix most seam leaks. If the metal is corroded through, replacement of that section is better.
